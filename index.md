# Site Info

## About

Hello! Welcome to my little corner of the web. This is basically where I typeset all the math I properly understand so I can have it nice and readable and accessible for whenever I need it. But I also want it to be open for reading to whoever wants to do so (a tribute to the miscellaneous resources I learned all this stuff from); hence the choice of medium.

Take note—Everything on here is subject to change. Case in point: This site's layout and approach used to be quite different. Some of the topics you see listed in the site nav are remnants of that (two-month) era. They read kind of like a diary; they're rather tedious, both to read and to write; and they rely on results and definitions this site (and, in many cases, its author) has yet to formalize. Basically, I'm not one hundred percent happy with them. But I won't be able to revise them until I can get a satisfactory foundation, which could take a minute, and in the meantime, they're still pretty good for many purposes. So, I'm keeping them on here but marking their titles with asterisks, and same for anything else I transfer in that I'm not yet one hundred percent happy with but that's still pretty good.
